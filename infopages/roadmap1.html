Defense Against Stagflation

Why we tilted the IRA further toward TIPS and International:

TIPS (SCHP): Unlike regular bonds, these adjust with the CPI. If inflation stays "sticky" at 3%+, your principal is protected.

Intl (VXUS): A hedge against a stalling US economy. If the US market corrects or the Dollar weakens, international diversification provides the offset.

Duration Note: SCHR has a ~5-year duration. Holding through 2031 effectively "guarantees" the yield regardless of rate volatility.
